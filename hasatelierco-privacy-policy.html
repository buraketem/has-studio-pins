Privacy Policy

Has Atelier Studio · HasAtelierCo · Last updated: October 6, 2026

Has Atelier Studio is a private, internal tool operated by HasAtelierCo, a small handmade fine jewelry shop (etsy.com/shop/HasAtelierCo). It is used only by the shop owner to publish and manage Pins of the shop's own products on the shop's own Pinterest account. It is not offered to the public or to other Pinterest users.

What data the app accesses

Through the Pinterest API, the app accesses only the HasAtelierCo Pinterest business account: its boards, its own Pins, and analytics for its own Pins (for example impressions, saves and outbound clicks). The app does not access, collect or store data about other Pinterest users.

How the data is used

The data is used only to create, schedule and organize the shop's own Pins and to understand which of the shop's own Pins perform well. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising profiles.

Storage and security

Access tokens and Pin information are stored locally on the shop owner's computer and are not shared. Tokens can be revoked at any time from the Pinterest account settings, which immediately stops the app's access.

Personal data

The app does not collect personal data from visitors or Pinterest users. Pins link to the shop's own Etsy listings; purchases happen on Etsy and are covered by Etsy's Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

Disconnecting the app from the Pinterest account removes its access. Locally stored Pin information can be deleted by the shop owner at any time. To request deletion or ask a question, email us at the address below.
